# TadkaNewz Punjabi News Portal

## Goal
Build a complete, launch-quality Punjabi digital newspaper experience using the requested red, white, and black identity. Match the reference’s general editorial density and information architecture without copying its branding, copy, or proprietary assets.

## Pages and navigation
- Create a shared publication header with breaking-news strip, prominent TadkaNewz wordmark, top-news teasers, category navigation, scrolling ticker, search, and polished mobile menu.
- Build a content-rich homepage at `/` with the lead story, four secondary stories, latest-news grid, category-led editorial sections, advertisements, community writing callout, and professional footer.
- Build SEO-friendly article pages using category and slug URLs such as `/entertainment/gulab-sidhu-new-song`, plus sample Punjab, sports, technology, and business articles.
- Create `/search` with a working keyword search and category/type filters.
- Create `/write` as a polished editorial prototype with title, category, cover upload preview, summary, content, tags, draft, preview, and publish interactions.
- Create an author profile page with biography, social links, article count, and published-story feed.
- Add supporting About, Contact, Advertise, Privacy, Terms, and Cookie pages so every footer and navigation destination works.

## Editorial experience
- Use varied, locally bundled/generated editorial imagery for Punjab, music, sport, technology, business, agriculture, lifestyle, and entertainment; avoid repeated or hotlinked images.
- Populate the publication with realistic Gurmukhi headlines, timestamps, summaries, bylines, tags, captions, and article copy.
- Build the requested article composition: category, large headline, byline and dates, share/bookmark/like controls, featured image, readable body, subheadings, quote, related links, secondary image, and 16:9 video treatment.
- Add a compact latest-news sidebar, standard ad placeholders, ranked trending list, two-column “ਇਹ ਵੀ ਪੜ੍ਹੋ” cards, and mixed “ਤਾਜ਼ਾ ਅਤੇ ਦਿਲਚਸਪ” feed.
- Make search, filters, menus, copy-link, share actions, bookmark/like controls, ticker, author navigation, and editor preview behave convincingly in the browser. Draft/publish actions will be an on-page prototype rather than an account-backed newsroom system.

## Visual direction
- Create a strong original TadkaNewz wordmark: “Tadka” in brand red and “Newz” in near-black, with compact Punjabi publication detailing.
- Use Noto Sans Gurmukhi, white newsprint surfaces, near-black typography, red rules and active states, restrained gray dividers, minimal rounding, and subtle shadows only where hierarchy needs them.
- Keep the desktop canvas around 1240px with a roughly 69/31 article-and-sidebar split; transform this into a purpose-built two-column tablet and single-column mobile reading flow.
- Preserve newspaper density while maintaining readable 18–20px article text, generous line height, strong subheads, stable image ratios, and accessible contrast/focus states.
- Add restrained transitions and ticker motion with reduced-motion support.

## Search and discoverability
- Add unique Punjabi/English title, description, Open Graph, canonical, Twitter-card, and page-type metadata to every content page.
- Add Article and author structured data where appropriate, semantic headings/landmarks, descriptive image alternatives, clean internal linking, and index-friendly category/slug URLs.
- Keep the site ready for a future content service while using a shared local editorial dataset for this visual build.

## Validation
- Check the homepage, article reading flow, search/filter flow, editor preview, author page, and all navigation destinations end to end.
- Verify desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts for clipping, overlap, typography, navigation behavior, sidebar reordering, and image loading.
- Confirm the final preview has no build, runtime, console, or broken-link errors.
